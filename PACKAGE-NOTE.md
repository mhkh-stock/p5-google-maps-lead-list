# PACKAGE NOTE — Fiverr-ready (P5: Google Maps Lead List)

**Gig title:** I will build you a verified lead list of businesses in your city and niche, ready to call

**One-line pitch:** Stop building call lists by hand. I pull every
business in your area and niche into one sortable sheet — name,
phone, website, address, source link — deduped and checked, so your
team calls instead of searches.

## What's included in every tier

- Excel lead sheet (Table + filters) with a Summary sheet
- Source link per lead, so any entry can be checked in one click
- Dedupe by business object, not by name — branches stay separate leads
- A short report: coverage per niche, how many leads carry phone/website, what is missing and why
- Sample shown here (74 Gulberg leads, 1 Nov 2026) is the exact
  template a real order starts from

## What the buyer sends

The city/area, the niche(s), and — for Premium ratings — their own
Google Places API key. Scraped Google Maps data is never offered:
ratings come from the Places API, contacts from public sources.

## Delivery promise

Basic in 2 days, Standard in 3, Premium in 5 — from the day the
area and niche list is received. Premium refreshes are scheduled,
not one-off.
